# Changelog

All notable changes are recorded here. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/). Before 1.0, a minor version may change a skill's behavior.

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- `webnovel-workflow` 0.2.0: resuming in a new conversation is now a defined routine, and the archive format is strict (axu-novel 4.0 archives are no longer supported). Each session reads the status file and its new handover section (what the author said that isn't in the archive yet), then runs `novel.py check` and `novel.py brief`. `check` is read-only and exits 1 on errors: status that disagrees with the files, a write-back left in progress (listing which records changed since the adoption snapshot), missing chapters, outline foreshadowing IDs the records lack, missing alias, state or outline index lines, and a dead or missing character in the next chapter's outline unless it is marked `特殊出场`; files past their size caps are warnings. `brief` writes the chapter's context pack (outline, handover, end of the previous chapter, recent timeline, the entries the chapter mentions by name and alias, unrevealed truths, dropped settings) with a manifest of what wasn't found, and refuses without an indexed outline. `adopt` now owns the progress fields: it refuses an out-of-order chapter or one adopted while a write-back is open, snapshots the records, advances 已采用到, clears the candidate pointers and opens the write-back; new `done` closes it only when check is clean, the timeline has the chapter, the handover reaches it and every name the chapter's outline listed has an entry, then writes the next step. Context is read in three layers with a do-not-read list; the opening interview is saved question by question; each file has a soft size cap and an archiving path. `evals/` holds eight resume scenarios, a fixture builder and the results of a first run with fresh sessions (four scenarios, all passed after the fixes listed there).
- `webnovel-workflow` 0.1.0: a long-form Chinese web-novel workflow adapted from 阿序AI's axu-novel 4.0 (CC BY-NC 4.0, kept for this folder). The craft material is the original's. Changed: checkpoints come in two tiers, so the author is stopped only for direction and is handed at most three decisions with a recommended default, while quality checks run through with a one-line report; the opening interview has a stop condition and works from what the author has read; power-system and golden-finger checks moved into a module that is read only for books that have one; each chapter's self-check goes to a fresh reader who sees the text and a fact list but not the outline; a standard-library tool (`novel.py`) creates the archive, files adopted chapters without deleting drafts, counts characters against the target and diffs the author's edits; voice is anchored to a chapter the author picked instead of only the previous one; volume close adds a rebuild-from-text audit of the records and a check for tics; leftover stubs and stale progress states from the original are removed. The archive layout is unchanged, so axu-novel 4.0 projects carry over. Not yet run on a real book.
- `miao-yanliang-macro-lens` 0.1.0: unofficial macro framework drawn from Miao Yanliang's public books, reports and interviews, for monetary policy, the dollar, gold, the renminbi, China's low inflation and AI-and-inflation questions. It analyzes with the framework instead of speaking as him, separates the durable mechanisms from dated opinion snapshots that must be re-checked, gives every judgment a horizon, conditions, a failure signal and a baseline, and ends with the strongest counter-argument. It gives no trade advice. Opinion snapshots run to 2026-09.
- `douyin-finance-koubo` 0.1.0: writes Douyin finance and business 口播 scripts in Mandarin. It lists what the most-watched takes already say and requires at least two pieces of material they lack, or it proposes a narrower angle instead of writing. The insight must reach the second layer of the mechanism and pass five written tests, and the script hands the viewer a reusable judgment tool shown on a second case. Retention is designed as a chain of questions with a first payoff inside 20 seconds and the main reversal at 40–50%. Drafting starts from a five-sentence spoken version, and the structural tells of generated text are ruled out. Cold reads for drop-off, takeaway, follow and voice run before delivery, and a failed takeaway or expert read sends the work back to research. It includes a standard-library lint for written-not-spoken tells and compliance flags, never invents people or first-person experiences, and gives no trade advice. Compliance facts are dated 2025–2026.
- `narrative-dcf-valuation` 0.2.0: the book's case-study routine is now required, not just the formula: a timeline of implied m at past highs and lows against the index, the consensus revision path, the one leading variable behind revisions, terminal revenue × margin with an EVA check, a calendar of dated binary events, and cross-listing spreads read through the auction-pricing theory. Reports must end with an over/under-valuation call, a fair terminal profit and m, and the contradictions found (none forced). New references: `cases.md` (each book case as a reusable rule) and `sources.md` (where to find each kind of data). A pre-delivery self-check recomputes every figure and checks probability consistency. The calculator adds `rebased` (for a one-year earnings jump) and `scenarios` (probability-weighted) modes. Company-specific risk goes into scenarios rather than also into r. After a blind test (fresh agents on WuXi AppTec and Zijin Mining, judged by an independent reviewer): the rebased `scenarios` mode is the single main basis and averaging two bases is forbidden (`fair` now reports the terminal that PE1 × E1 really implies); timeline rows carry E0, E1 and the implied terminal profit, and highs and lows must be real price extremes; contradictions must pass an admission test (would the price move if it were true?); A/H spreads are compared with peer stocks, not the market-wide index; near-term listing shocks are kept apart from distant legal bans; cyclicals get their own bottom rule and a commodity-and-FX main variable; growth paths are recompounded against the terminal revenue; `cases.md` warns that a stable PE means a stable m, not a missing re-rating.
- `narrative-dcf-valuation` 0.1.0: values any listed stock with the closed-form DCF from 穿透叙事：寻找A股投资解析解 (Zou Peixuan, 2026). It reverses the price into the implied earnings ceiling m at a discount rate r, turns a judged m and r into a fair forward PE and value with probability-weighted cases, handles companies with no earnings, and diagnoses A-share auction-pricing drift. A standard-library calculator reproduces the book's Tables 1-1, 1-2, 1-3 and 1-8 exactly, and uses the exact perpetual-growth formula instead of the book's lower-r shortcut, which overstates (56.2× vs 66.3× at m=10, r=10%, g=2%). It gives no trade advice.
- `video-director` 0.5.0: a "Readable on a phone, consistent across frames" section with four rules for the defects blind reviewers flagged most in test runs: a 32 px size floor for every word or number the viewer reads, labels placed in code so close neighbours don't collide, every number computed once and shown at one precision, and keyframes taken at settled moments, never mid-word. The phase-2 strip check now looks for all four. In a blind A/B (6 runs per version, two judges) it removed captions caught mid-word and contradictory numbers; overall scores were level with 0.4.0, and small text and crowded labels remain.
- `rescue-a-stock` 0.1.0: reads where China's A-share market stands in the state rescue and clearing cycle. It collects live data with a standard-library tool, classifies interventions by who pays, sets a stage per segment with rule-based confidence caps, and explains the reading as a causal chain with a test for every link. Includes a playbook of base rates from a study of 64 interventions across eight cycles, and dated facts (verified 2026-11). It gives no trade advice.
- `oss-mentor`: maintainer-partner skill with safety rules, decision rights, hobby/serious/org profiles, phase diagnosis, an upstream-contribution playbook and dated platform facts (verified 2026-10).
- `oss-design`: tiered product, tech and UI/UX design skill with convention-first guidance, drawn-theme variety, blind pairwise critique, a fresh-agent first-success test and accessibility gates.
- `video-director`: turns inspiration into finished videos. It locks the format first, asks at most 3 questions and shows three 10-second direction demos. It builds the video as code under a minimal render contract, so any renderer works. It defaults to continuous, one-take composition with on-screen words instead of slides and synthetic voiceover. Tuning notes become data edits on a shared cue sheet for picture and sound, and render caching is per segment. A blind 7-dimension reviewer loop runs before delivery. Includes a kit (`render.mjs`, `timeline.py`, `audio.py`, `qc.py`, `frames.py`, an optional shot runtime, and one-take and shot-based starters) and dated facts (verified 2026-10).
- `video-director`, after its first real use (a 3:42 explainer):
  - The user confirms what the video says: a story card (promise, takeaways, every on-screen line, numbers with sources, what's left out) ships with the direction demos, and the build waits for it. The rough cut is a real checkpoint for pieces over 60 s or that explain, argue or sell.
  - An audience pass: a fresh agent plays 2–3 target viewers, one of them skeptical, on the script and the rough cut. It reports where they'd drop off, which takeaways land, and what feels preachy or untrue.
  - Music by default: a new `music.py` composes a bed that follows the cues. Missing music is a QC warning, and lowering the loudness target to pass QC is ruled out.
  - Token budget: images are inspected by short-lived subagents rather than the main conversation, and the review pack is capped at about 20 images whatever the length. Round-2 A/B compares paired stills instead of two full packs.
  - Kit:
    - JPEG frame capture by default (about 5× faster on textured pages).
    - Workers default to half the cores.
    - Tool changes no longer invalidate the picture cache.
    - A final 4×-oversampled limiter for click-heavy mixes.
    - Frozen picture is a warning, not a failure.
    - Caption reading-speed checks.
    - New `frames.py tile` and `pair` subcommands.
  - Preflight checks the login shell's PATH before reinstalling tools.
  - Directions are five sets of 3 keyframes (hook, key moment, end), not three 10-second demo videos. One is led by the references, two come from random draws, one is a deliberate extreme and one is a best-in-class conventional take. A 10-second motion test is rendered only for the chosen direction, and only when its appeal depends on motion.
- `video-director` 0.2.0, after analysing a code-made rhythm lesson (9½ min, synthesized grooves locked to an on-screen beat ruler):
  - A Lesson format for chaptered explainers (3–10 min): a cold open, chapters that each add one concept, teaching pages that alternate with full examples, and a persistent on-screen instrument drawn from data.
  - `music.py` plays the content's own patterns: `audio.grooves` windows with 13 presets (heartbeat, march, waltz, rock, disco, funk, trap, reggae, shuffle, bossa, tresillo, dembow, 7/8) or custom step lanes, with swing and per-window tempo. Every hit is written to `audio/events.json` so the picture draws from the same data. The bed drops out under each window.
  - A fact check before the audience pass for anything that explains or teaches: a fresh agent rates every claim ok, overstated, unsupported or wrong, and the gate allows no wrong or unsupported line on screen.
- `video-director` 0.3.0, recorded sound effects (method adapted from [vibe-motion/sound](https://github.com/vibe-motion/sound)):
  - New `sfx.py`: searches Freesound through its official API, re-ranks relevance results by downloads and shows votes, license and the uploader's description; `get` downloads HQ previews in parallel, converts them to 48 kHz WAV and logs source, license and credit in `licenses.json`, refusing BY-NC and Sampling+ unless the piece is non-commercial; `measure` reports length, transient, ring and energy band so a popular clip is checked before it's trusted.
  - SKILL.md prefers recorded effects picked on evidence (downloads, rating count over average, the description as listening) over synthesis, which stays as the fallback without a key and as a sub layer.
  - The mixer takes a list of layers per cue (`{path, gain_db, len}`), aligns each layer's transient on the cue for any audio format, and trims and fades a long ring. `timeline.py check` validates layers and warns when a Freesound clip isn't in `licenses.json`.
  - The Freesound key lives in one fixed place, `~/.config/video-director/freesound-key` (mode 600, outside every repo); `sfx.py key` says whether it's set without printing it, and `sfx.py key --set` saves one from stdin. `FREESOUND_API_KEY` overrides it for one run.
  - The kit's gitignore covers downloaded audio under `audio/` and the search index.
  - `audio.py mix` names missing audio files instead of failing inside ffmpeg.
