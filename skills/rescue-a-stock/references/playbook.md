# Rescue playbook

History for reading the current cycle. It comes from a study of 64 coded interventions across eight cycles, with prices through 30 October 2026. Returns start from the T-1 close, so T+1 is the T0 close. The figures are fixed history: don't edit them unless the event study is re-run.

**Limits.** There are eight cycles and events cluster, so effective sample size sits nearer 8 than 64. Cycles were chosen because they crashed, so pre-rescue events are followed by falls partly by construction. Intent labels are judgement. Size isn't modelled, and returns exclude dividends. Treat these as evidence ranked by strength, not as probabilities.

- **Money works, and fades in large caps.** Active SSE T+5 is +3.97pp above same-depth controls (p<0.001), but +1.59pp at T+20 (p=0.32). September 2024 lifts the mean: the T+5 median is +2.06%.
- **Small caps collect later.** SSE 50 minus CSI 1000 is +1.30pp at T+1 and -7.41pp at T+20 after Active events (p=0.007).
- **Early talk is ineffective, not a trap.** Early-Performative T+1 is +0.01%. At T+20 it matches same-phase days (-3.30% against -3.87%, p=0.77).
- **Real traps attach elsewhere (exploratory).** Early-Passive events show a CSI 300 trap rate of 78% against 31% (n=9). ChiNext Early-Performative shows 86% against 27% (n=7, not significant).
- **Talk works after money.** Late-Performative SSE T+20 is +2.41%, early is -3.97%. "Late" partly means after a recovery has begun.

A trap here means a gain of at least 2% by T+10, then a close at least 5% below T-1 by T+60.

- **Tolerance tightened.** The drawdown before major money fell from about -30% to about -11%.
- **Speed beats depth.** Three of four money events since 2024 followed a five-session fall of about 6% or worse. September 2024 is the exception: flat prices and a macro window.
- **The forced seller decides the rung.** Margin drew balance-sheet money, while pledges drew credit-side relief.
- **Ladder, ordered by who pays:** verbal (nobody), administrative limits (issuers, shorts, funds), stamp duty and margin ratio (MoF, brokers), Huijin and SOE money (quasi-fiscal), PBOC lines (PBOC, banks), then personnel and Politburo language.
- **Why they wait:** agencies contest who pays, waiting lets prices clear and saves capacity, and macro mandates can bind outright (inflation in 2008, deleveraging in 2018).

Other precedents for a trigger: average margin maintenance of 226% when the CSRC flexed liquidation lines on 5 February 2024, about 1,000 limit-downs on 2 February 2024, and a one-day SSE 50 minus CSI 2000 gap of 10.7pp.

## Causal channels

- **Signal.** Beliefs move without much cash. September 2024 rose 27% before the swap took applications.
- **Flow absorption.** The state takes the other side. RMB 1.2-1.7tn in 2015 (estimate), about RMB 820bn in cash in 2024. It can fail: October 2026 saw RMB 110-170bn (estimate) and new lows.
- **Forced-seller relief.** Best micro evidence. In 2018 disposals were only about RMB 9.8bn by December, so the state froze the trigger rather than buying out the problem.
- **Macro.** Some turns owe little to equity rescue. In 2008, five RRR hikes came while the index halved.

Leverage isn't needed for a 70% fall, as 2008 showed. That separates price-only crashes, where the state signals, from funding-linked ones, where it pays.

## Checking claimed buying

Treat a flagged day as state buying only when share creation appears across several same-index funds on a down day, and treat its size as an upper bound. Turnover alone is not enough.

| Claim | Evidence | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Feb 5, 2024 | 512100 turnover z 47, shares +132.8% in a day, filing row dated that day | Strongest on record |
| Oct 23, 2023 | 510300 z 3.6, but small-cap ETFs shrank | Large caps only |
| Oct 2018 | Fund exits matched CSF rises: an internal transfer | Not verified |
| Sep 24-Oct 8, 2024 | z up to 21, but Huijin rows unchanged | Spike was not the state |
| Jan 2026 selling | Shares -16% to -41% in 10 days | Direction verified |

**The National Team is a leveraged collar, not a floor.** Huijin's short-term parent borrowing rose from RMB 60bn to 623bn in 2024, and its ETF book reached RMB 1.526tn by end-2025. It sold into strength in 2014-15 and again in 2026 (at least an 87% cut across 11 funds in H1, an upper-bound calculation, with no filing naming the seller). Selling on rallies earns a return by stabilising, so "stabiliser" and "profit-seeker" imply the same trade on exit.

## Who pays for restrictions

Restrictions move losses rather than remove them. The July 2015 suspensions left 1,442 of 2,781 firms untradeable. On 5 February 2024, quant sell curbs coincided with CSI 2000 -9.49% against SSE 50 +1.18%. The link is circumstantial, and no data show how much was blocked. Leverage clears slowly: 63% of peak margin was still there at the July 2015 policy bottom. Dependence has grown too, with investors selling into the close on 27 October 2026 when state buying failed to appear.

## Worked example: 2 November 2026

**Reading.** Large caps at Stage 3-4, broad small and growth at Stage 4. Headline Stage 4, Retest. Confidence Low: the 2 November PBOC meeting had happened and its outcome was unknown. A readout would lift the cap to Medium at best, since one more close could cross a gate and snowballs, off-exchange funding and DMA were unmeasured.

**Facts.** SSE -14.2% from peak at 3,638, ChiNext -43.8%, CSI 1000 -29.3%, CSI 300 -20.6%. Margin -22.7% from its peak. Pledges RMB 2.26tn against 4.30tn in October 2018. IM discount about 7-9% annualised, against about 15% in August. PPI +5.0%, CPI +0.9%. The Fifth Plenum didn't mention markets.

**Chain.**

```text
Stress plus -11% → first money, July  [D]  about RMB 60bn disclosed  |  breaks if: flows predate the fall
First money fades → new lows, second round  [D]  SSE 4.4% below the July base by Oct 30  |  breaks if: lows hold for 20 sessions
Inflation and contained funding stress → PBOC keeps to bonds, relending tweaked  [I]  PBOC warned against a backstop illusion  |  breaks if: PBOC names equities
```
**Weakest link:** the last one, because the PBOC has not stated this motive. **Rival chain:** an externally timed AI repricing that policy can't reach, which would show up if A-share lows track SOX regardless of state action.
