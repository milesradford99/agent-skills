# Douyin finance 口播 (v5): 值得关注的那种

Use this when the user wants a Douyin (抖音) finance, macro, policy or business-commentary 口播 script written, rewritten or audited. Talk to the user in their language; write the script in Mandarin.

## The standard

A script passes only if a viewer gets four things, in this order of priority:

1. **收获:** they understand something they didn't, and leave with a tool they can use on the next piece of news.
2. **留人:** at every moment they have a reason to keep watching, and that reason is the reasoning itself.
3. **关注理由:** they conclude "this person sees things I can't, and will again".
4. **人话:** it sounds like one person talking, not like text being read.

Each has a gate below. When a gate fails, go back to the stage that feeds it. Polishing sentences never fixes thin material, and most weak scripts are weak before the first sentence is written.

## Three failures this skill exists to prevent

All three happened on real topics with earlier versions.

**怪味 (formula).** A strawman hook ("十万亿化债，债一分没少？"), invented stakes, per-capita arithmetic posing as relevance, timed "这跟你有什么关系" bridges, template phrases, fragments manufactured for rhythm, numbers without mechanism.

**乏味 (research note).** 40+ numbers each with a spoken source, no role for the viewer, linear exposition, no stance, no image, and the best news hook deleted out of caution.

**幼稚 (competent but shallow). This is what v4 produced.** The signs:
- The 原来如此 is something a smart viewer could guess from the headline.
- The mechanism stops at the first layer. It says what happened and the direct cause, and never asks the next "那为什么".
- Nothing in it required work. Every fact is on the first page of search results.
- The viewer learns about this one event and gets nothing to carry to the next one.
- The persona is a costume: attitude sprayed on top, with no view that comes out of the material.
- The form is too tidy: every paragraph the same size and closing on a 金句, symmetrical sentences, a lifted ending. It reads as written.

The line that still holds: **fake is banned; energy is required.** Real suspense built from the reasoning, a clear stance, humor, analogy and surprise are expected. False urgency, strawmen, hype words and suspense the content can't pay off are out.

## Inputs

Ask only for what's missing, in one message:
1. Topic or news peg, audience, target duration. Default 4–6 minutes; the depth of the material decides, and a piece is shortened by removing whole sections, never by thinning every paragraph.
2. Source material, or permission to research.
3. **The creator's raw take:** their gut reaction to the topic in two or three sentences, typed or dictated, unpolished. This is the single best input for stance and voice.
4. **Voice:** 2–3 transcripts of their own videos (best), or one of the persona presets.
5. **账号母题:** the one lens this account applies to everything (for example "每件事都算一遍谁出钱谁拿钱"). If there isn't one, propose two or three after Stage 3 and let the user pick.
6. Speaking rate (default ~230字/分).

If the user can't be asked, proceed with the best-fitting preset and say what was assumed.

## Stage 1: Topic and increment

Answer in writing before anything else:

1. **Why now:** the news peg. Keep it even when it's market heat; state it as fact, never as a price call.
2. **公共知识:** search what the most-watched videos and most-read articles on this topic already say. List the three or four points everyone makes. These may appear in the script as setup, and none of them may be its payoff.
3. **What people believe,** and what is wrong or missing in it. That gap is the hook.
4. **The viewer's role:** where they honestly sit in the story (their attention, deposits, fees, job, city). If there is none, the hook rests on curiosity alone. Do not invent a stake.

## Stage 2: Find the 硬料

Depth comes from material other people didn't go and get. Research until there are **at least two** of these that the 公共知识 list lacks:

- **原始文件里的细节:** a line in an annual report note, a prospectus risk factor, the exact wording of a policy document, a tender notice, an exchange inquiry letter.
- **一笔自己算的账:** unit economics worked from disclosed figures, shown step by step, with assumptions stated.
- **对照组:** the same industry with the opposite outcome, the same policy last time, another country's version.
- **反常点:** a fact the popular explanation can't account for. This is usually where the real mechanism is hiding.
- **当事人原话:** what a named participant said on the record, with date and venue.

**数据纪律:** for each number, its definition, source and date; measured vs estimated; never mix definitions.

If two can't be found, say so to the user and propose a narrower angle where an increment exists. Do not write the script anyway.

## Stage 3: The insight, and the bar it must clear

Write the analysis memo in Chinese:
- **机制链:** at least three causal links, each with what makes it hold (a rule, an accounting identity, pricing logic, an incentive) and each actor's goal.
- **账:** who gains, who pays, through what channel.
- **边界:** what it doesn't explain, the steelmanned counterargument, what would prove it wrong.

Then climb the ladder and write one line per rung:

| Rung | What it is |
|---|---|
| L1 现象 | What happened |
| L2 直接原因 | What the news says caused it |
| L3 机制 | Why it had to go this way: incentives, constraints, the accounting |
| L4 第二层 | What the mechanism implies that isn't obvious: who really bears the cost, why the obvious fix fails, what it sets up next |
| L5 可迁移 | The pattern, stated so it applies to other cases |

**The script's payoff must be at L4, and it must hand the viewer L5.** L1–L2 are setup and should be over quickly.

Distill:
- **原来如此:** one sentence, under 30字.
- **带走物:** the tool the viewer keeps. One of: a judgment rule ("以后看到X，先看Y"), a calculation they can now do, or a changed way of looking. Never a buy/sell conclusion.
- **迁移案例:** a second, different case where the 带走物 works, in one or two sentences. It goes into the script; it is the proof that the tool is reusable.
- **可转发的一句话,** true and mechanism-based, not a slogan.
- **吵点:** the real disagreement the comments will argue about.

**Insight gate. All five must pass, in writing:**
1. **标题测试:** could a viewer guess the 原来如此 from the headline alone? If yes, it's L2. Keep digging.
2. **追问测试:** ask "那为什么……" three times in a row. The memo must answer each.
3. **内行测试:** would someone who works in this industry say "对，而且外面很少有人讲到这一层"? If they'd say "这谁不知道", fail.
4. **反驳测试:** the strongest objection is answered, and the answer will be spoken in the script, not buried.
5. **迁移测试:** the 带走物 works on the 迁移案例 without bending.

A failure sends the work back to Stage 2.

## Stage 4: Why would they follow

A follow is a bet on future videos. "点个关注" does nothing; three things in the script do, and each must be shown, never claimed:

- **功夫:** one or two details that make visible the work the viewer wouldn't do themselves (the footnote, the worked account, the comparison nobody ran).
- **镜头:** the 账号母题 used openly as the way in. The viewer should be able to imagine it pointed at the next topic.
- **坦白:** one honest line about what is still unclear or where this reading could be wrong. Calibrated doubt is what makes the rest believable.

No requests for follows, likes or shares. The ending may point at the next question this piece really opens, only if the creator intends to make that video.

## Stage 5: Structure and retention

Pick the skeleton the topic naturally has. Suspense is legitimate when it IS the reasoning.

| Skeleton | Use when | Shape |
|---|---|---|
| 破案 Whodunit | "Who profits / who pays / why did X happen" | Mystery → suspects ruled out one by one, each by a surprising fact → culprit → mechanism → twist |
| 揭秘反转 Myth-buster | A widely held belief is wrong | What everyone thinks → the fact that breaks it → the real mechanism → what it means |
| 跟着钱走 Follow the money | A flow or chain | One yuan, one ad view or one product through each hand, a reveal at each stop |
| 演化史 Escalation | Something that changed over time | Each stage is a turn, stakes rising |
| 双城记 Two paths | Two similar actors, opposite outcomes | Same start → the hidden difference → consequences |
| 一个人 Character | A person or company embodies the mechanism | Scene → decision → the system behind it → zoom out |

Never announce the skeleton ("咱们按破案的路数来"). A refrain can work once or twice; by the third time it is a formula.

**Retention design.** Write the outline as a chain of questions. For each beat note what the viewer is wondering; the next beat answers it and opens the next.

- **Hook.** One or two sentences that put the gap on the table. The creator's preferred form is a direct, conversational question to the viewer ("你相不相信……"); a scene or a number that doesn't fit also works. Length is whatever can be said in one natural breath or two. Write 3–5 candidates and test each: is it true, is it about me, does it show I'm missing something, can the body pay it off in full?
- **First payment within about 20 seconds:** a real, surprising fact. It tells the viewer this isn't clickbait and buys patience for the long reasoning.
- **One main question stays open until about 80%.** Smaller questions close along the way, each closing by opening the next.
- **The turn at 40–50%:** where the answer the viewer has settled on gets overturned. This is where mid-length videos lose people; put the strongest reversal here, not at the end.
- **One carrier:** a single concrete case, object or yuan carries the whole piece. Abstract statements ride on it; they don't float.
- **Density:** every sentence is a new fact, a step in the reasoning, or an image. Delete restatement and recap paragraphs. If two sentences say the same thing, keep the plainer one. No stretch over about 40 seconds without something new.
- **Number budget:** about 8–12 spoken numbers for 4–6 minutes, rounded for the ear, each given a comparison ("从一百块掉到五块"). One worked calculation may use more. Spoken attribution only where a claim is surprising, three or four times at most. Everything else goes in the source list.
- **Ending:** the 原来如此, the 带走物 in the viewer's own words, the 迁移案例, then stop. No lift into grand meaning. A question for the comments only if it is the real 吵点.

## Stage 6: Voice

A voice profile built from the creator's transcripts overrides everything in this section. Without one, use a preset and say plainly in the delivery that the result will sound like a competent stranger until the creator's own words go in.

**Stance comes from material.** For every opinion or jab in the script there is a fact in the memo that produces it. The creator's raw take sets the direction. Attitude with no fact under it gets cut.

**Presets** (moves and stance, never phrases; never present the script as a specific real creator):
- **A. 毒舌拆解官:** sees through the incentive chain and finds it absurd. Deadpan irony, a short verdict after a long setup, analogies from games, office life and consumption. Humor aims at systems, never at ordinary people's losses. Risk: cynicism replacing analysis, sarcasm about named companies tipping into defamation.
- **B. 好奇探索者:** "I assumed X, then found Y". Delight at an elegant mechanism, warm second person. Risk: wonder without judgment; still take a side.
- **C. 宏大叙事者:** places the story in a cycle or a historical pattern, zooming from one company to the system. Risk: big claims without an anchor fact.
- **D. 冷面算账人:** the calculation is the drama. One worked example, dry understatement at the result. Risk: number parade.
- **E. 纪实讲述者:** opens on a real person, scene or moment, then the system behind it. Risk: invented people. Real, sourced cases only; hypotheticals labelled "假设".

In every voice: analogies where they make a mechanism click, followed by the mechanism itself; rhetorical questions only where the viewer is really asking them.

**How to draft so it comes out spoken:**

1. **饭桌版 first.** Before drafting, write how the creator would explain this to a friend who asked over dinner "这事到底怎么回事": five or six sentences, no notes. That is the spine and the register.
2. **Talk it forward.** Expand the 饭桌版 beat by beat. Do not convert outline headings into paragraphs, and do not carry the memo's wording across; the memo is written language.
3. **Spoken Chinese:** topic first, then comment. Short verbs. Sentences may run on with 然后, 结果, 你看. Repeat the same plain word where an essay would vary it. 语气词 (吧、嘛、呢、啊) where a speaker would have them. Concrete nouns over category words.
4. **Uneven, because the content is uneven.** The key mechanism gets ten sentences; a minor step gets one. Paragraphs differ in length. Two or three lines in the whole script are quotable; the rest just say the thing.
5. **An aside is allowed** when the material offers one: a detail that amused or annoyed. One real precision correction ("准确说是……") is fine. Neither is added for effect.

**Structural tells of generated text. None may remain:**
- three parallel clauses in a row (排比三连), or matched pairs (对仗);
- every paragraph closing on a short punchline;
- 自问自答 ("为什么？因为……") more than twice; 不是……而是 more than once;
- an ending that rises into meaning ("这或许才是……");
- abstraction standing in for the thing ("这背后是一套……的逻辑") with the thing never said;
- announcing before saying ("接下来这点很关键");
- generic stand-ins (小王、某公司) where a real case exists;
- even paragraph sizes, even sentence lengths, even enthusiasm;
- stacked 成语 and written connectives (然而、此外、因此、与此同时);
- both-sides hedging that never lands on a view;
- filler tics (说白了、你想啊) scattered at regular intervals. Tics come only from the voice profile, at the frequency measured there. Performed casualness is as detectable as stiffness.

**Honest first person.** Never invent an experience, a person or a conversation. Where a personal moment would help, leave 【此处可加你的亲历：……】. Process lines ("我去翻了它的招股书") are allowed only for sources actually in the fact pack, and each is tagged 【录前请亲自核一眼：来源N】 so the sentence is true when the creator says it.

## Stage 8: Cold reads

Run each as a separate pass. If a fresh-context subagent is available, give passes 1–3 and 5 to one that has not seen the memo; otherwise reread cold, script only. Flag only, then do targeted rewrites, at most two loops.

1. **划走测试.** A viewer scrolling at night. Mark every point they'd leave and why. Check: first payment by 20s, the turn at 40–50%, any stretch over 40s with nothing new.
2. **收获测试.** After one read, without looking back, the reader writes (a) what they now understand and (b) what they'll do differently the next time they see similar news. Compare with the 原来如此 and 带走物. A mismatch or a vague answer is a **failure of Stage 3 or 5**, not of wording.
3. **关注测试.** "Would I follow, and which sentence made me?" The reader must point to the 功夫, the 镜头 and the 坦白. If they can't find one, it isn't there.
4. **内行审稿.** A senior researcher and a strict finance editor, with the fact pack: claims without mechanism, missing links, ignored incentives, incomparable or misdefined numbers, the unanswered objection, factual errors. Name the single most important omission.
5. **真人测试.** The reader marks every sentence that sounds written rather than said, or generated, and names the tell from the Stage 6 list. They also mark performed casualness, strawmen, invented stakes and hype. Then the whole script is read aloud at speed: any line that trips the tongue or that nobody would say to a friend gets rewritten.
6. **信息守恒.** Every fact, number and case traces to the memo and fact pack. Claim strength and priority haven't drifted; essential caveats remain. Unsourced items become [待核实].
7. **合规.** Rules below.
8. **Lint.**

If pass 2, 3 or 4 fails, go back to the stage it points to. Do not rewrite sentences to get past it.

## Deliver

- Final script, plain text with no markdown, dashes or emoji, plus estimated duration.
- **Scorecard:** one line for each of 收获, 留人, 关注理由, 人话, saying what in the script delivers it and what is still weak. Be blunt; do not round up.
- One line each: 原来如此, 带走物, 迁移案例, the shareable line, the 吵点, the voice used.
- **硬料清单** and the **字幕/置顶评论来源清单:** every number with its source and date.
- What was cut, [待核实] items, 【亲历】 and 【录前请亲自核一眼】 slots, compliance REVIEW items.
- **The creator's ten minutes** (below).

## The creator's ten minutes (tell the user every time)

The last stretch of 真人感 can only come from the person on camera.
- Read it aloud once, recording on the phone. Wherever you stumble or the wording isn't yours, say it your own way and keep what you said.
- Add your reaction in the one or two places you actually had one.
- Open each tagged source and look at it, so the process lines are true.
- Fill in or delete every 【亲历】 slot.
- After one listen, could a viewer explain the 原来如此 to someone else?
- Put the source list in the subtitles or the pinned comment.
- Send back the transcript of what you actually recorded. It becomes the voice profile, and the next script starts closer.
- After posting, compare 5-second retention, the retention curve, the drop around the midpoint, follows per view and comment topics across scripts, and keep what works on your account.

## Compliance (China, 2025–2026)

**Rules:**
- 《抖音社区财经行业公约（试行）》 (16 Dec 2025).
- 《金融产品网络营销管理办法》 (effective 30 Sep 2026): non-institution staff may not market financial products by short video, and 荐股 is especially forbidden.
- CSRC Jiangsu: ordinary users may share personal views.
- CAC (Mar 2026): staged and AI-generated video must be labelled.

**What this means for scripts:**
- Market heat may be stated as a fact ("这阵子概念股涨得很猛"). Never predict prices, never say buy, sell or hold, never give targets or timing, and never imply returns. The 带走物 is a way of reading news, never a trading rule.
- No insider or 独家 framing, no off-platform diversion, no invented investment stories.
- Jokes about companies must rest on disclosed facts. Calling legitimate accounting 造假 or 猫腻 is a legal risk.
- Close with one natural spoken line: personal views, not investment advice.
- Flag to the user: whether the account needs certification for 专业财经内容, and the labelling rules for AI voice, digital humans and AI-generated content. Natural writing is a quality goal; it does not replace any labelling the rules require.

## Lint (hygiene only)

Write this to `koubo_lint.py`, save the script as `script.txt`, and run `python3 -I koubo_lint.py script.txt <target_seconds>`. FAIL means a hard problem. WARN means look, then decide in context. INFO is never a reason to edit. The lint catches surface tells only; it cannot judge depth, interest or voice. Those are the cold reads.

```python
#!/usr/bin/env python3
"""koubo_lint.py v5: hygiene + written-not-spoken checks for Chinese finance 口播 scripts.
Usage: python3 -I koubo_lint.py script.txt [target_seconds]"""
import re, sys, statistics

RATE = 230  # 字/分钟; replace with the creator's measured rate

SPOKEN_REGISTER = ["首先", "其次", "综上所述", "总而言之", "值得注意的是", "值得一提的是",
                   "需要指出的是", "不仅如此", "让我们", "在当今", "快速变化的时代",
                   "大家好", "希望对大家有帮助"]
WRITTEN_LINK = ["然而", "此外", "因此", "从而", "进而", "与此同时", "换句话说", "简而言之",
                "总的来说", "事实上", "由此可见", "不难发现", "毋庸置疑", "殊不知", "诚然"]
EMPTY_WORDS = ["至关重要", "深入探讨", "彰显", "标志着", "不可忽视", "深远影响", "赋能",
               "底层逻辑", "抓手", "格局", "未来可期", "拭目以待"]
AI_CLICHE = ["真相是", "答案很简单", "答案是", "细思极恐", "想象一下", "试想", "这背后",
             "背后的逻辑", "本质上", "归根结底", "更重要的是", "这意味着", "你没看错",
             "换个角度", "才是真正的", "远不止", "远比你想", "不妨", "要知道", "恰恰相反",
             "值得深思", "才刚刚开始"]
VAGUE_SOURCE = ["专家认为", "业内人士指出", "有分析认为", "据了解"]
TEMPLATE_LEAK = ["先说个词", "你就理解成", "这跟你有什么关系", "我的结论是", "先说结论",
                 "评论区说说", "评论区聊聊", "先别划", "问题来了", "第一个变化", "第二个变化",
                 "划重点", "敲黑板", "听懂了吗", "记住这句话", "一定要看到最后"]
TICS = ["说白了", "你想啊", "我跟你说", "说实话", "你注意"]
ATTRIB = re.compile(r"的数据|数据显示|报告显示|统计|据[^，。]{0,8}(报道|披露|测算)|引用的")
HYPE = re.compile(r"震惊|炸裂|惊人|可怕|恐怖|后悔|千万别|颠覆|暴击|血亏")
SMEAR = re.compile(r"造假|猫腻|割韭菜|骗局|收割")
BEG = re.compile(r"点个?关注|关注我|记得关注|点个?赞|一键三连|下期见")
GENERIC_PERSON = re.compile(r"小王|小李|小明|小张|小陈|老王|张三|李四")
PARTICLE = re.compile(r"[吧嘛呢啊呀呗哈哎欸]")
SELF_QA = re.compile(r"[？?]\s*(因为|很简单|答案|原因)")
COMPLY = {
    "疑似A股代码": r"(?<!\d)(00|30|60|68)\d{4}(?!\d)",
    "买卖/择时": r"买入|卖出|抄底|逃顶|上车|加仓|减仓|清仓|止盈|止损位|目标价",
    "收益承诺": r"稳赚|保本|保息|必涨|翻倍|躺赚|零风险|无风险",
    "内幕暗示": r"内幕|内部消息|独家爆料|小道消息",
    "站外导流": r"加V|加微|微信号|私信我|进群|扣1领|领资料",
}
NUM = re.compile(r"\d+(?:\.\d+)?%?|百分之[零一二两三四五六七八九十点百]+|[一二三四五六七八九十]+分之[一二三四五六七八九十]+|[一二两三四五六七八九十百千万亿]+(?:成|倍|块|元|万|亿|%)")

def hlen(s):
    return len(re.sub(r"[\W_]", "", s))

def bare(s):
    return re.sub(r"[\W_]", "", s)

def bigrams(s):
    s = bare(s)
    return {s[i:i + 2] for i in range(len(s) - 1)}

def lint(text, target_sec=None):
    out = []
    if "待核实" in text:
        out.append(("FAIL", "稿中仍有[待核实]"))
    if "此处可加你的亲历" in text or "录前请亲自核" in text:
        out.append(("INFO", "有亲历/核对占位，录制前补上或删掉"))
    body = re.sub(r"\[[^\]]*\]|【[^】]*】", "", text).strip()
    n = hlen(body)
    sec = n / RATE * 60
    out.append(("INFO", f"{n}字 ≈ {sec:.0f}秒 @ {RATE}字/分"))
    if target_sec and abs(sec - target_sec) > 0.1 * target_sec:
        out.append(("WARN", f"时长偏离目标{target_sec}秒超过10%：整段增删"))
    first = re.match(r"[^。！？!?\n]+", body).group()
    if hlen(first) > 40:
        out.append(("WARN", f"第一句{hlen(first)}字，确认一两口气能说完"))
    paras = [p.strip() for p in body.split("\n") if hlen(p) > 0]
    for p in paras:
        if hlen(p) > 180:
            out.append(("WARN", f"段落{hlen(p)}字无落点: {p[:16]}…"))

    # research-note signals
    nums = len(NUM.findall(body))
    budget = max(8, int(sec / 60 * 3))
    if nums > budget:
        out.append(("WARN", f"口头数字约{nums}个，超过预算{budget}：挑最有冲击力的，其余进来源清单"))
    att = len(ATTRIB.findall(body))
    if att > 3:
        out.append(("WARN", f"口头出处{att}处，研报腔：只在最意外的说法上提出处"))

    # written-not-spoken: structure
    contrast = (len(re.findall(r"不是[^。！？\n]{0,15}[，,]\s*(而是|是)", body))
                + len(re.findall(r"的是[^。！？\n]{0,15}[，,]\s*不是", body)))
    if contrast >= 2:
        out.append(("FAIL", f"「不是…而是」翻案句{contrast}处，最多留1处"))
    qa = len(SELF_QA.findall(body))
    if qa > 2:
        out.append(("WARN", f"自问自答{qa}处（为什么？因为…），最多2处"))
    if len(paras) >= 3:
        linked = sum(1 for p in paras[1:] if p.startswith(("但是", "但", "所以", "因此", "然而", "而", "可是", "于是")))
        if linked / (len(paras) - 1) >= 0.8:
            out.append(("WARN", "几乎每段都用连接词起头，过渡过于顺滑"))
    if len(paras) >= 4:
        pl = [hlen(p) for p in paras]
        if statistics.pstdev(pl) / statistics.mean(pl) < 0.25:
            out.append(("WARN", "各段长度几乎一样：重点给足篇幅，次要的一两句带过"))
        closers = 0
        for p in paras:
            ss = [s for s in re.split(r"[。！？!?]+", p) if hlen(s) > 0]
            if len(ss) >= 2 and hlen(ss[-1]) <= 12:
                closers += 1
        if closers >= 3 and closers / len(paras) > 0.5:
            out.append(("WARN", f"{closers}段以短句收尾，像每段配一句金句：全稿留两三句最硬的"))
    sents = [s for s in re.split(r"[。！？!?\n]+", body) if hlen(s) > 0]
    for a, b in zip(sents, sents[1:]):
        if hlen(a) >= 8 and hlen(b) >= 8:
            ba, bb = bigrams(a), bigrams(b)
            if len(ba & bb) / max(1, len(ba | bb)) >= 0.4:
                out.append(("WARN", f"相邻句高度相似: {a.strip()[:12]}… / {b.strip()[:12]}…"))
    if len(re.findall(r"[：:]", body)) >= 2:
        out.append(("WARN", "冒号多处，检查是否在用冒号引出结论"))
    clauses = [c for c in re.split(r"[，,。！？!?；;：:…、\n]+", body) if hlen(c) > 0]
    i = 0
    while i + 2 < len(clauses):
        a, b, c = (bare(x) for x in clauses[i:i + 3])
        if min(len(a), len(b), len(c)) >= 4 and a[:2] == b[:2] == c[:2]:
            out.append(("WARN", f"三连排比: {a[:8]}… / {b[:8]}… / {c[:8]}…"))
            i += 3
        else:
            i += 1
    for c in clauses:
        if c.count("的") >= 3:
            out.append(("WARN", f"长定语，耳朵跟不上: {c.strip()}"))
        if hlen(c) > 28:
            out.append(("FAIL", f"一口气说不完(>28字): {c.strip()}"))
        elif hlen(c) > 20:
            out.append(("WARN", f"偏长(>20字)，念一遍确认: {c.strip()}"))
    lens = [hlen(s) for s in sents]
    if len(lens) >= 8:
        if statistics.pstdev(lens) / statistics.mean(lens) < 0.45:
            out.append(("WARN", "句子长短太均匀，真人说话有长有短"))
        frag = sum(1 for l in lens if l <= 5) / len(lens)
        out.append(("INFO", f"≤5字短句 {frag:.0%}（仅参考）"))
        if frag > 0.2:
            out.append(("WARN", f"碎句占比{frag:.0%}，检查是否为节奏硬断"))
    part = len(PARTICLE.findall(body))
    if n >= 300 and part < n / 150:
        out.append(("WARN", f"语气词只有{part}个，像念稿：照着说一遍，把自然带出来的吧/嘛/呢/啊留下"))

    # written-not-spoken: words
    for w in SPOKEN_REGISTER:
        if w in body:
            out.append(("WARN", f"书面/念稿腔: {w}"))
    for w in WRITTEN_LINK:
        if w in body:
            out.append(("WARN", f"书面连接词: {w}"))
    for w in EMPTY_WORDS:
        if w in body:
            out.append(("WARN", f"空泛词: {w}"))
    for w in AI_CLICHE:
        if w in body:
            out.append(("WARN", f"生成腔常见说法: {w}"))
    for w in VAGUE_SOURCE:
        if w in body:
            out.append(("FAIL", f"模糊出处: {w}"))
    for w in TEMPLATE_LEAK:
        if w in body:
            out.append(("WARN", f"模板痕迹: {w}"))
    tic_n = sum(body.count(w) for w in TICS)
    if tic_n > max(1, n // 300):
        out.append(("WARN", f"口头禅类标记{tic_n}处，偏多"))
    for m in HYPE.finditer(body):
        out.append(("WARN", f"夸张词: {m.group()}"))
    for m in BEG.finditer(body):
        out.append(("WARN", f"求关注/求互动: {m.group()}，关注理由要靠内容挣"))
    for m in GENERIC_PERSON.finditer(body):
        out.append(("WARN", f"泛化人物: {m.group()}，换成真实有出处的案例或标明假设"))
    for m in SMEAR.finditer(body):
        out.append(("REVIEW", f"定性用词，确认有公开事实支撑且不涉及具体公司: {m.group()}"))
    out.append(("INFO", f"问句{body.count('？') + body.count('?')}个"))
    if not re.search(r"不构成投资建议|个人看法|个人观点|不是投资建议", body):
        out.append(("WARN", "缺口语化风险提示"))
    for name, pat in COMPLY.items():
        for m in re.finditer(pat, body):
            out.append(("REVIEW", f"合规人工复核[{name}]: {m.group()}"))
    return out

if __name__ == "__main__":
    txt = open(sys.argv[1], encoding="utf-8").read()
    tgt = int(sys.argv[2]) if len(sys.argv) > 2 else None
    res = lint(txt, tgt)
    for lvl, msg in res:
        print(f"{lvl:6} {msg}")
    sys.exit(1 if any(l == "FAIL" for l, _ in res) else 0)
```

## Voice profile (when the creator's transcripts are provided)

From 2–3 transcripts of the creator's own videos, or the transcript of what they actually recorded from an earlier script, write a profile covering:
- how they open and move between points;
- how they state views and hedge;
- their humor and analogy sources;
- common words, 语气词 and phrases with rough frequency;
- typical sentence and paragraph lengths, and how uneven they are;
- things they would never say;
- 3–5 short verbatim excerpts, for cadence only;
- anti-patterns: how an imitation of them would go wrong.

When the creator sends back a recorded version, diff it against the delivered script: every change they made is a rule about their voice. Add those to the profile. The profile replaces the persona preset. Offer to save it.
